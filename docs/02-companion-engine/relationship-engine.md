Version: 1.0

# Introduction

Relationship Engine is the intelligence layer responsible for managing the evolving relationship experience between a user and an AI Companion.

Relationship Engine answers the fundamental question:

"How does this relationship develop over time?"


PAO is not designed around isolated conversations.

The value of PAO comes from continuous interaction and accumulated relationship context.


# Purpose

Relationship Engine exists to create a structured relationship experience by managing:

- Interaction history.
- Familiarity.
- Trust progression.
- Shared experiences.
- Relationship milestones.
- User preferences.
- Companion adaptation.


# Core Principle

## Relationship Is A System

A relationship experience must not depend only on random AI generation.

Relationship development must be:

- Structured.
- Measurable.
- Consistent.
- Explainable.
