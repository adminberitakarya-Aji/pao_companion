# Introduction

Timeline Engine is the intelligence layer responsible for managing the chronological history of interactions, events, and experiences between a user and an AI Companion.

Timeline Engine answers the fundamental question:

"What has happened before?"


A companion is not only defined by who they are.

A companion is also defined by the journey they have experienced with the user.


# Purpose

Timeline Engine exists to create continuity by managing:

- Historical events.
- Relationship milestones.
- Important moments.
- Companion experiences.
- Chronological context.


The engine transforms isolated interactions into a continuous journey.


# Core Principle

## Time Creates Continuity

A companion experience becomes meaningful when past events influence future interactions.
