Version: 1.0

# Introduction

Conversation Engine is the intelligence layer responsible for generating natural, personalized, and context-aware conversations between users and AI Companions.

Conversation Engine answers the fundamental question:

"How should this companion respond?"


The engine transforms:

- User input.
- Companion identity.
- Relationship context.
- Historical context.

into a personalized companion response.


# Purpose

Conversation Engine exists to provide:


- Natural conversation.
- Personality-consistent responses.
- Context awareness.
- Relationship-aware interaction.
- AI provider orchestration.
