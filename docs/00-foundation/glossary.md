# Introduction

This document defines the official terminology used throughout PAO COMPANION.

All product, engineering, AI, and design discussions should use these definitions as the source of truth.


# Core Terms


# PAO COMPANION

## Definition

PAO COMPANION is an AI Companion platform designed to create personalized, long-term digital companion experiences.

PAO focuses on identity, relationship, continuity, and personalization.

PAO is not a chatbot system.

---

# AI Companion

## Definition

An AI Companion is an artificial intelligence entity designed to provide ongoing personalized interaction with a user through identity, memory, relationship, and conversation.

## Core Characteristics

An AI Companion has:

- Identity.
- Personality.
- Memory.
- Relationship state.
- Interaction history.
- Consistent behavior.


---

# Companion

## Definition

A specific AI entity created and experienced by a user.

Example:

"Luna is my PAO Companion."

A companion represents one unique digital personality.


---

# Companion Instance

## Definition

A deployed individual companion belonging to one user.

Each companion instance has:

- Unique identity.
- Unique configuration.
- Unique relationship history.
- Unique timeline.


---

# Character

## Definition

The designed identity of an AI Companion.

Character defines:

"Who is this companion?"


Includes:

- Name.
- Appearance.
- Personality.
- Background.
- Communication style.
- Preferences.


---

## Definition

The complete structured representation of a companion's identity.

Character DNA is the foundation used by all PAO systems.
